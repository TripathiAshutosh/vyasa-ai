# Vyasa AI Assistant
### Chhatrapati Shahu Ji Maharaj University, Kanpur — Intelligent PhD Admission Helpdesk

---

## What is Vyasa?

**Vyasa** is CSJMU's AI-powered assistant that answers PhD admission queries **instantly, accurately, and around the clock**. Instead of searching through lengthy brochures or waiting for office hours, applicants simply ask — and Vyasa responds in seconds, citing the exact official document and page number behind every answer.

Vyasa is trained **exclusively on the University's official PhD Admission Brochure and PhD Ordinance (2026–27)** — it never guesses, never improvises. If a question falls outside official documents, it politely redirects the applicant to the admissions office.

---

## Key Features (Live Today)

**🎓 24×7 Admission Assistance**
Eligibility criteria, entrance exam pattern, required documents, fee structure, refund policy — answered in natural language, in seconds.

**📄 Every Answer is Verifiable**
Each response cites the source PDF and page number — applicants can trust what they read.

**🛡️ Grounded & Safe by Design**
Vyasa answers only from official university documents. Off-topic or uncertain queries are respectfully declined — no misinformation, ever.

**📊 Admin Analytics Dashboard**
The University team gets real-time insight into how applicants engage:
- How many users are asking, and how usage grows over time
- Peak hours of activity — when applicants need help most
- Query segmentation — which topics (eligibility, fees, exams, documents) dominate, helping the University spot information gaps and improve outreach

**🔄 Self-Service Document Updates**
New notification released? The admissions team drag-and-drops the updated PDF — Vyasa learns it in under a minute. No developer needed.

**🌐 Seamless University Integration**
Embeds directly into the university website with native CSJMU branding — as a page, an iframe, or a floating chat widget.

## The Road Ahead 🚀

Vyasa is just the beginning. The vision: **an AI companion for CSJMU's entire research ecosystem.**

- **R&D Cell Assistant** — extending Vyasa to serve the complete Research & Development Cell
- **Research Intelligence for Scholars** — ask Vyasa about the University's research legacy: which papers and patents were filed, trends year by year, department-wise output, and emerging topic areas — all conversationally

*Vyasa — named after the great compiler of knowledge — is set to become the single intelligent gateway to everything CSJMU knows.*
